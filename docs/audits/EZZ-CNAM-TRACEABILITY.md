## Proposed domain and contracts (design recommendation)

New clinic-scoped module cnam should own administrative insured/beneficiary relationships, guardian authority evidence, practitioner credential versions, affiliation effective-date history, rule references/verification events, dossiers, signed-act/invoice links, source-graded dossier events, issuance snapshots, numbered handoffs, copy/correction events, filing evidence and consented optional outcome records. Composite tenant keys, FORCE RLS and append-only evidence must follow existing patterns. Patient clinical text is not copied into general audit logs; record identifiers/content digests with least-privilege access.

Proposed API contracts must define version/expectedRevision, actor derived from session, stable 403/404/409, transaction/idempotency semantics, act uniqueness under concurrent issue, restricted binary evidence access and explicit state/source fields. The UI cannot set verified=true. A source grade must identify evidence type, issuer/source, effective/observed date, reviewer and provenance; staff acknowledgement cannot manufacture external verification. Treatment and issue dates are distinct immutable fields. A reasoned correction produces a new event/document retaining the original.

Dossier amount fields separate clinic charged fees, conventional reference amounts (unknown until verified), proposed reimbursement (unavailable unless complete current rules) and optional reported/evidenced patient outcome. No dossier event posts ordinary CNAM reimbursement to clinic receipts, cash, revenue or receivables. Any exceptional direct-payer contract is separately explicit and disabled without evidence.

## Non-negotiable V1 boundaries

1. “Estimation indisponible” unless all current validated code/tariff/rate/age/coverage/remaining-entitlement inputs justify a number. The historical 2021 percentages and 2024 ceilings are not a ready-to-use current reimbursement formula.
2. No stored dentist signature/stamp image. Authenticated electronic authorship metadata does not replace actual physical signature and stamp on official paper.
3. V1 produces clearly labelled preparation information for official paper forms. No claim that a software facsimile is official or accepted. Direct preprinted alignment/calibration stays V1.1.
4. No assumed official API, scraping, patient impersonation or invented online eligibility/status. Conditional V2 requires documented authorised integration.
5. Patient pays clinic; ordinary reimbursement to patient is separate. Act/teeth/practitioner reconciliation does not require private fees to equal reference tariffs.
6. Multi-practitioner attribution is required; automatic per-dentist splitting is not justified by unverified official form rules.
7. Privacy, minors, current form/codes/tariffs/deadline applicability and retention require independent qualified verification before real dossiers. Unknowns must be visible and do not authorise invented mandatory fields or diagnoses.
