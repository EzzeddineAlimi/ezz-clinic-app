## Interpretation and scope safeguards

- U062 and U071 are concrete policy mismatches: current refund is OWNER+reauth and finance.review OWNER-only, whereas approved workflows describe dentist approval/report viewing. Do not resolve by broadly granting clinical data to owners or money actions to every dentist. GPT/founder must define role combinations and sign-off semantics first; identity.ts and M-264/265 are source evidence.
- U059 requires a financial model change, not a permissive overpayment toggle. Current integrity.ts explicitly asserts full single-visit allocation. Amend that invariant with credit/deposit and allocation tests in the same reviewed slice.
- U073 and U106 include two or more dentists/receptionists in one clinic now. Multi-location remains later. Generic clinic tenancy does not establish scheduling race safety or full staff workflow acceptance.
- U075 authorises a feature scope, not creating a backup schedule during this audit. No schedule/task was touched.
- U036's mention of U101 does not silently promote the separate later privacy-request register; a minimal correction handoff must preserve current V1 requirements and be specified by GPT.
- Clinical findings, signed care, patient payment, period cash count and external card evidence are distinct facts. No status above makes theft impossible or verifies wholly unrecorded cash.
